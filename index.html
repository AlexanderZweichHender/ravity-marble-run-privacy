Privacy Policy

Gravity Marble Run for iOS · Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

Gravity Marble Run does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The game works offline. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, locations, contacts, device identifiers or usage analytics.
  • We do not use advertising networks, analytics services or third-party tracking tools.
  • The game does not connect to any server operated by us.

Data stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps your unlocked levels, stars, best scores, coins, purchased backgrounds, completed challenges, daily bonus timer and sound settings in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the game. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, a copy may be included in that backup under your own Apple account.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Deleting your data

Open the game and go to Settings → Reset Progress, or simply delete the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
