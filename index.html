Privacy Policy

Gravity Marble Run for iOS · Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

Gravity Marble Run is a casual puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or precise location. To keep the game stable and to understand how players find and use it, the app uses a small number of third-party services that collect limited technical and usage data, described below. We do not sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings.
  • Identifiers — an app-instance or installation identifier, and the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS permission prompt.
  • Usage data — gameplay events such as levels started and completed and session length.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports and performance data.
  • Attribution data — information about how the app was installed (for example, which advertising campaign led to the install).

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — app usage statistics and crash reporting. Firebase privacy information.
  • AppsFlyer — install attribution and marketing measurement. AppsFlyer Privacy Policy.

These providers process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies.

Tracking and your choices

The app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the Advertising Identifier. If you decline, the IDFA is not collected. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, a copy may be included in that backup under your own Apple account.

Data retention

Analytics and attribution data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and according to the retention settings of the providers listed. Crash reports are retained for a limited period by Firebase Crashlytics.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data related to you, or to object to its processing. To make a request, email us at the address below. Deleting the app removes all data stored locally on your device.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
